How Ironday works

Ironday helps you build a gym habit. Plan what you train each day, log every set, eat for your goals, and get a nudge when you need one. This guide walks you through it in about five minutes.

1

Sign in with Google

Open Ironday and tap Continue with Google. Your account keeps your workouts, meals and goals in sync across your phone and computer.

  • Your data is saved to your account only. Nobody else can see it.
  • It also works offline. Changes sync when you're back online.

Plan your weekly split

Open Workouts and choose what you train each day, for example Monday: Shoulders & Triceps. Ironday fills in proven exercises with sets and reps, and you can change any of them.

  • Pick from Chest & Triceps, Back & Biceps, Legs & Glutes, Arms, Full Body, Cardio & Core, Rest, or make your own.
  • Set a reminder time for each day.
  • Busy day? On Today, tap Change today's workout to switch just today, or every week.

Log your workout

On Today, tap Start workout. Enter the weight and reps for each set and tick it when it's done.

  • A rest timer starts when you tick a set (60, 90 or 120 seconds) and beeps when it's time to go again.
  • Ironday shows what you lifted last time, so you know what to beat.
  • Tap Finish workout to save it. New personal bests get a shout-out, and your streak grows.

Track food and water

Open Nutrition and tap + Add on breakfast, lunch, dinner or snacks. Pick a common food from the list or add your own with its calories and protein.

  • See calories, protein, carbs and fat against your daily targets.
  • Tap the glasses to log water. One glass is about 250 ml.
  • Change your targets any time under Goals › Daily targets.

Set your goals

On Goals, tap + New goal. Ironday tracks progress from what you log, so you don't have to update it yourself.

  • Body weight: log your weight and watch the trend line.
  • Lift target: for example "Bench press 80 kg", checked against your heaviest set.
  • Workouts per week, or a custom goal like "Run 5 km".
  • Earn badges for your first workout, a 7-day streak, hitting protein and more.

Get smart reminders

Tap the bell at the top left of Today to see your alerts. Ironday reminds you about what matters right now.

  • Your workout time, 15 to 90 minutes before.
  • Streak protection in the evening if you haven't trained yet.
  • Protein, lunch and water nudges, goal milestones, and a preview of tomorrow.
  • Choose which ones you want under Settings › Notifications. Turn on phone notifications there too.

Keep a copy in Google Sheets (optional)

In Settings › Google Sheets, tap Connect Google Sheets. Ironday creates a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive and keeps it updated.

  • Tabs for workouts, sets, food, water, body weight, goals and your weekly plan.
  • Ironday can only open the files it creates, not the rest of your Drive.
  • Prefer a file? Settings › Export & import downloads a spreadsheet or a full backup.

Your privacy

Your data is yours

Each person's data is stored in a private record that only their own Google sign-in can open. Other Ironday users can't see or change it.

You're in control

Export your data any time, erase it, or delete your account from Settings › Privacy & security.

Shared screen?

Hide your name on the home screen and stop keeping a copy on the device in Settings.

Questions

Is Ironday free?

Yes. Sign in with your Google account and start using it.

Do I need internet at the gym?

No. Once you've opened Ironday, you can log workouts offline. Everything syncs when you're back online.

Do reminders work when the app is closed?

Reminders appear while Ironday is open or running in the background on your phone. Turn on phone notifications in Settings › Notifications.

What units does Ironday use?

Weights are in kilograms and water is counted in glasses of about 250 ml.

Can I use it on my phone and computer?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and your data follows you.
